Nashville, I-24 westbound, 30 November 2022, 06:00–10:00 CST, four mainline lanes over the instrumented miles. Mean speed from the I-24 MOTION trajectory instrument in 60 s × 100 m bins. Time runs left to right; position runs upward in the direction of travel, so every band sloping down-right is a stop-and-go wave travelling backwards through the traffic.

Nothing here is animated by hand. The ring frames are trajectories written by Eclipse SUMO with the Intelligent Driver Model, replayed with linear interpolation between the 0.5 s samples; the wave is emergent (no seeded perturbation). The controlled vehicle runs FollowerStopper (Stern et al. 2018). Source and data: abc000cool/flowstate-v2.
