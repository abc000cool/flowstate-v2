Nashville, I-24 westbound, 30 November 2022, 06:00–10:00 CST, four mainline lanes over the instrumented miles. Mean speed from the I-24 MOTION trajectory instrument in 60 s × 100 m bins. Time runs left to right and position runs upward in the direction of travel, so every band sloping down to the right is a stop-and-go wave travelling backwards through the traffic. This is measured data, not a simulation.

Nothing here is animated by hand. The ring frames are vehicle trajectories written by Eclipse SUMO with the Intelligent Driver Model, replayed with linear interpolation between the 0.5 s samples. The wave is emergent: no perturbation is seeded. The controlled vehicle runs FollowerStopper (Stern et al. 2018). Every number on this page is computed from the recorded runs (data pack built by scripts/website_sim_pack.py).
